# Paying for custom fee service appointment

Migrated from `appointment-payments.feature` scenario 7
"paying for custom fee service appointment".

## Steps

1. Take payment for the price-varies appointment via Point of Sale: set the
   price to **$90**, apply the **TStax (13%)** tax and a **10% (percentage)**
   discount, then record the sale.
2. Payments Received shows **Payment for Sale #1 - service**.
