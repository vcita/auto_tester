# Sales Widget — Empty State

## Objective
On a fresh account with the new dashboard, the Sales widget shows its empty state and the "Start accepting payments" call-to-action opens the payment setup wizard.

## Prerequisites
- Isolated account with the `new_dashboard` flag, logged in (from `_setup`).
- No payments have been set up or recorded yet (fresh account).

## Steps
1. Open the new dashboard.
2. Observe the Sales widget.
3. Click the "Start accepting payments" button.

## Expected Result
- The Sales widget renders its empty state (empty-state title, empty payment list, and the CTA button).
- Clicking the CTA opens the "get paid online" payment wizard.

## Context Updates
- None.
