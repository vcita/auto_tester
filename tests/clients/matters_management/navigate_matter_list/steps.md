# Navigate Matter From List

## Objective
Verify a user can click a matter from the matter list and the page title updates to
show the selected matter.

## Prerequisites
- From `nest_matter`: on a matter page whose matter list contains "contact client"
  and "matter client" (the contact and its nested matter).

## Steps
1. In the matter list, click the matter named "contact client".
2. Verify the page title shows "contact client".

## Expected Result
- The page title shows "contact client".

## Context Updates
- None.
